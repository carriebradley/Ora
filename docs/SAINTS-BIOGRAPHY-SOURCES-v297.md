# Saint of the Day: December 28–31, 2026 (v297)

These original, brief biographies are linked to the Catholic appointment data stored in Ora.

- Dec 28: The Nicomedia martyrs. Edmonton's title states **two thousand**, while the Byzantine Orthodox biographical text reports **twenty thousand**. The app card identifies the group without stating an unverified exact number. The difference is left visible in provenance and the original appointment title.
- Dec 29: The Holy Innocents of Bethlehem, commemorated as a group. Historical numerical estimates are not stated.
- Dec 30: Edmonton's dated source returned HTTP 404. **No Ukrainian saint biography is promoted from an unverified date.** Melkite Newton appoints Melany (Melania), with Anysia as another saint.
- Dec 31: Edmonton appoints Melania the Roman. The Melkite December 31 page appoints the leave-taking of the Nativity without a distinct named saint; Ora does not fabricate a card.

# Roman saints and November 2026 appointments

This correction addresses the disappearing **Saint of the Day** card on Roman weekdays and a missing mandatory memorial. It does not make a Sunday saint into an appointed observance.

- **November 22, 2026:** Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe is a solemnity (white). Cecilia's fixed November 22 date is outranked. The Roman Saint of the Day card intentionally has no displaced saint; the existing *From the saints* quotation still appears.
- **November 23:** Monday of the last week in Ordinary Time remains a **green weekday**. Saint Clement I, Saint Columban, and (in the United States) Blessed Miguel Agustín Pro are **optional** memorials. The saint card can feature a sourced biography of Clement and explicitly says “Optional memorial,” without switching the day's liturgical vestment, prayer plan, or main Today title.
- **November 24:** Saint Andrew Dung-Lac and Companions are a **mandatory red memorial**, not an ordinary green weekday. The Roman Today card and calendar should agree.
- **November 25:** The green weekday and optional red memorial of Catherine of Alexandria remain distinct.

**Authoritative appointment evidence:** [USCCB U.S. Liturgical Calendar 2026](https://www.usccb.org/resources/2026cal.pdf), November 22–25; [USCCB November 23 daily readings and options](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/112326.cfm); [USCCB November 24 memorial](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/112426.cfm).

**Biographical witnesses:** Clement: [Benedict XVI general audience on Clement of Rome](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/audiences/2007/documents/hf_ben-xvi_aud_20070307.html); Andrew and Companions: [Franciscan Media](https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-andrew-dung-lac-and-companions/); Catherine: [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03445a.htm).

**Boundaries:** This is dated U.S. Roman calendar metadata and restrained UI matching, not a claim of licensed Mass/Office texts or a review of every 2026 date. The remaining source-fidelity and device acceptance tasks stay open.

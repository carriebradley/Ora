### Editorial rules

- Only the **first verified named commemoration** for the selected tradition receives a biography. The rest remain in the full Calendar.
- The same historical subject may use shared copy across traditions only after their independent appointments have been verified.
- Claims peculiar to hagiographic traditions are explicitly identified as traditional accounts. Avoid conflating the prophet Obadiah with the named court official as an established historical certainty.
- On an ordinary weekday or a feast of the Lord / Theotokos without a named saint, do not fabricate a saint card.
- No portraits are inserted in this batch: suitable image licenses and accurate identity must be checked independently.
- If a match fails or a source is uncertain, keep the existing truthful placeholder instead of showing the wrong saint.

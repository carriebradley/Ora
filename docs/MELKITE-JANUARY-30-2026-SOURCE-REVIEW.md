# January 30, 2026 - Melkite Three Holy Hierarchs typicon anomaly

Reviewed: November 16, 2026.

## Verified dates and source distinction

- January 30, 2026 was a **Friday** (Gregorian civil calendar, ISO weekday 5).
- The Diocese of Newton's 2026 annual calendar (January, printed page 3) celebrates the Fathers Basil the Great, Gregory the Theologian, and John Chrysostom on January 30, with John 10:9-16 (Orthros), Hebrews 13:7-16 (Epistle) and Matthew 5:14-19 (Gospel).
- The Diocese also describes the January 30 feast in its catechesis: https://melkite.org/faith/sunday-scriptures/a-trinity-of-hierarchs-january-30 .
- Its January monthly typicon is published at https://melkite.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/January-2026.docx. The Ora importer detected a heading that prints **Thursday** for **January 30, 2026**.
- Annual calendar page: https://melkite.org/2026-diocese-of-newton-calendar .

## Decision

The fixed feast and reading references are independently supported by the annual diocesan calendar. A typo in the monthly typicon's weekday does **not** automatically identify the text beneath that heading as valid appointed hymns for Friday, nor does the annual calendar establish the precise service type or parish time.

Accordingly, Ora keeps the date's calculated ordinary Chrysostom service, **does not** promote its service verification to `verified`, and retains the January 30 hymns in the existing import quarantine. The collapsed **Readings & propers** section now explains the discrepancy and links the original monthly typicon for review.

The Ukrainian and Ruthenian calendars remain separate. No hymn texts, local books, Scripture passages, or other dates were changed.

## Resolution gate

Unquarantine only after inspecting a corrected diocesan typicon or receiving independent diocesan confirmation that the actual hymn text under the mislabeled heading is appointed for Friday January 30. A correct feast date alone is insufficient.

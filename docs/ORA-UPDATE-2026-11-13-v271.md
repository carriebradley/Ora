# Ora v271 — Byzantine companions and remaining Roman text gaps

## Added Byzantine content

Both Ukrainian and Melkite companions retain their distinct calendars and links. The shared additions are identified as historical English for personal prayer, not as either church's current approved English Horologion.

- Small Compline: the invitation to worship, It Is Truly Meet, More Honourable than the Cherubim, the prayer to the Mother of God, the full prayer before sleep, the concluding Marian invocation and the Trinity protection prayer. Restore the full doxology before Our Father in the Trisagion chain. Keep the appointed canon and kontakion at their proper positions, with source links. The final petitions and forgiveness remain in the selected church's book.
- Vespers: full Psalms 140, 141, 129 and 116, the complete Vouchsafe prayer, and the Trisagion/Our Father after Simeon's canticle. Mark the intervening variable portions at their positions. Clergy litanies are omitted in this personal-prayer companion.
- Orthros: Psalm 50 and the Praises (148–150), with the canon and other variable sections marked at their positions.
- All eight Resurrection troparia, selected only for Sunday Orthros or the preceding Saturday Vespers with a dated, verified tone for the chosen church. Suppress this addition at Pascha, Palm Sunday, during the Pentecostarion before All Saints, and fixed feasts of the Lord. Sunday evening does not reuse Sunday morning's hymn.
- Bright Week continues to omit the ordinary psalms and fixed additions.

Historical text: G. V. Shann, *Euchology* (1891), printed pages 12, 61, 77–80, 82–84 and 417–425; [CCEL transcription](https://ccel.org/ccel/shann/euchology/euchology.ii.html). Small Compline's closing rubric on page 84 refers explicitly to the prayers printed under Great Compline. Drop-cap initials, page breaks, a known encoding error, the split word “incorruptible” and an obvious missing period are repaired. Display capitals are normalized without rewriting prayers. The reproducible importer exports only these bounded public-domain passages.

Structure corroboration: [Edmonton domestic-prayer guide](https://eeparchy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/For-the-Domestic-Church-2020UKRCATH1.pdf) gives the reader opening and omits clergy litanies; [UGCC catechism](https://eeparchy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/ugcc-catechism-english-20200924-2nd-ed-final-for-print.pdf), §§541–545, describes the daily cycle. [Melkite Eparchy of Australia](https://www.melkite.org.au/prayer) describes the same cycle. These factual witnesses do not authorize copying unrelated modern translations. Full canons, stichera and seasonal orders still require the churches' books. Newton's monthly typicons remain valuable dated sources for hymns and readings, rather than complete Horologion services.

## Roman calendar-transfer gaps

Added three Second Sunday after Christmas offices for fixed January 6 Epiphany and three Ordinary Sunday IX offices for Thursday Corpus Christi. The public-domain short reading 1 John 5:20 is now bundled. Usual Week I short readings for Ordinary IX Vespers are corroborated against the corresponding Week V DivineOffice witnesses; the alternate site's longer readings are identified as longer alternatives, not falsely described as printing errors.

The complete section audit now runs all eight Epiphany/Ascension/Corpus settings. All 462 Morning/Evening Prayer appointments per setting, November 12, 2026–June 30, 2027, have the audited prayer sections and an appointed short-reading passage available. This is section coverage, not certification of every word, all four hours or the complete annual cycle.

## Office of Readings

Added seven approved First Edition ICEL nonbiblical readings and their responsories: Cyril of Jerusalem for Advent I Sunday; Gregory Nazianzen for Advent I Tuesday; Basil for Holy Tuesday; Augustine for Holy Wednesday; Conrad of Marburg for Elizabeth of Hungary; John Chrysostom for Andrew; Ambrose's letter for his memorial. Ancient/medieval underlying works are public domain; the approved English translations remain ICEL's property and are used under its noncommercial-website policy. Required notices appear at both ends of each added reading pane, with no claimed ICEL endorsement. Bible bodies, hymns and recordings are excluded from this import.

All 64 previously bundled readings remain unchanged; the bundle now contains 71. The reproducible index inventory finds 562 unique second-reading references, 71 available and 491 still unbundled. This counts possible temporal/sanctoral references, not days of a single calendar year. The annual reader remains incomplete. See office-of-readings-availability.json.

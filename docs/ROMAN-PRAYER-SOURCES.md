# Roman prayer sources and labels

This pass advances [issue #165](https://github.com/carriebradley/Ora/issues/165). The January 11, 2027 Roman Morning and Evening Prayer renderers were compared with the [First Edition Morning Prayer](https://divineoffice.org/ord-w01-mon-mp/) and [Evening Prayer](https://divineoffice.org/ord-w01-mon-ep/) witnesses. Ora chooses the appointed Psalm 5 and Psalm 29, 1 Chronicles 29 canticle, Psalms 11 and 15, and Ephesians 1 canticle for those Hours. Its psalmody uses Douay–Rheims wording.

| Screen and section | Source or display treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Morning and Evening Prayer, Psalmody | Douay–Rheims psalms and biblical canticles. The compact credit sits under the Psalmody heading. Sourced antiphons and optional psalm-prayers remain distinct from the biblical text. |
| Morning and Evening Prayer, short reading and hymn | When the appointed biblical excerpt is available, Ora labels its translation separately. Some reading references use a whole-verse excerpt or another identified translation. A traditional chapter or historical English hymn carries its own label. |
| Morning and Evening Prayer, Gospel canticles and other prayers | Benedictus and Magnificat use ICET wording. Available ICEL antiphons, responsories, intercessions and appointed concluding prayers are credited in the Office acknowledgment. Historical or devotional fallbacks have separate headings. See the [text inventory](roman-office-text-permissions.md). |
| Night Prayer | Psalms and the short reading are already marked Douay–Rheims. The hymn identifies its historical English translator. The closing acknowledgment credits the ICET Canticle of Simeon and the sourced Office prayers. |
| Office of Readings | The index explains its scope. The first reading uses Douay–Rheims. Appointed second-reading excerpts display their individual translations or a notice when unavailable. A saved Office book opens from local storage. |
| Roman Mass and Readings | The private Mass preview draws its text from the selected saved Missal. The Readings tab shows imported USCCB text or date-specific references and links as available. Feed references and a saved book are separate sources. |

The credit labels the Scripture in Psalmody while keeping each prayer's existing source information in place. Browser coverage checks its location and singular display in Morning and Evening Prayer, and checks the existing Night Prayer and Office of Readings paths. The audit documents display and translation provenance for the compared sections. Exact wording against a current approved printed edition remains a separate comparison.

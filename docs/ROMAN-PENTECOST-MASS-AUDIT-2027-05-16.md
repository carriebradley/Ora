# Roman Pentecost Mass audit for May 16, 2027

The [dated USCCB Lectionary entry](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/051627.cfm) provides separate Vigil, Extended Vigil and daytime links. The [Roman Missal reference for Pentecost](https://www.liturgies.net/Liturgies/Catholic/roman_missal/pentecostmass.htm) presents the forms separately. Compare any exact wording and later emendations against a current approved U.S. edition.

## Saved Missal preview

The date-only Mass preview selects the Pentecost day heading. In an EPUB with the Vigil and daytime forms inside one Pentecost section, the headings can appear as subheadings rather than separate EPUB sections. The previous preview extracted propers across both forms, so the Vigil and daytime Collects could appear together in the same field. The updated preview uses only the blocks under the single explicit daytime subheading. A separate daytime heading also matches when the EPUB table of contents gives it Pentecost context. Multiple eligible matches or a clearly marked Vigil-only section stay available in the original saved book for manual navigation.

The May 16 calendar and invented EPUB fixture exercise this behavior through the Mass view. The test checks that the day's Collect, entrance and prayer after Communion appear, the Vigil Collect is excluded and the Gloria and ordinary Creed are available. It checks the reader's use of source headings and the layout of the propers. The Readings tab, the Saturday evening calendar transition, the in-app Sequence and exact current-edition prayer wording remain separate audit items. For the Vigil, open the original saved book and choose the appropriate form.

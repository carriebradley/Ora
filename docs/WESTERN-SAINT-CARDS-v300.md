# Ora v300 — Western source-backed Saint of the Day cards

## Appointment sources and policy

Ora's Roman and Traditional/Sarum calendars remain the sole authority for which feast is actually selected on each date. A short biography must match the primary observed title. Outranked commemorations stay in Calendar, not the Saint of the Day card. Dates in the outside historical references are **not** substituted for Sarum local dates (notably Linus).

**Historical uncertainties:** Leonard's popular Life is a later witness and is not independently reliable. Chrysogonus's link with Anastasia comes from a later legend. Linus's martyrdom is traditionally claimed but not historically established. The source summary distinguishes these from firmer historical facts.

All card summaries are original, fewer than 50 words, and linked to an external source. Shared Clement and Catherine biographies reuse the existing verified Byzantine biographies, while preserving the distinct Traditional dates.

# Western Saint of the Day coverage and primary-observance rule (v298)

The **Roman** and **Traditional/Sarum** cards now read only the primary observed liturgical title. A saint displayed solely in the calendar's `also` array as outranked by a Sunday or higher feast is not promoted into the Saint of the Day card. **Byzantine** remains distinct: its typika can keep a named primary saint inside `also` alongside the day heading.

The new common Western biography records cover Cecilia (Nov 22), Andrew (Nov 30), Nicholas (Dec 6), Lucy (Dec 13), Stephen (Dec 26), John the Apostle (Dec 27), and the Holy Innocents (Dec 28). Roman-specific additions: Leo (Nov 10), Martin (Nov 11), Mother Cabrini (Nov 13), Francis Xavier (Dec 3), and John of the Cross (Dec 14); Ambrose's already sourced short biography is reused on Dec 7.

All summaries are under 50 words and have a separate source link. The Roman and Sarum calendar appointments, priorities, and festival dates are **not** altered.

# Social Teaching cards and complete passages

Each Social Teaching card category now has a distinct border color and icon. Icons match their own card's borders. The daily quotation uses quotation marks, creation a leaf, economic justice scales, the radical voice a flame, charity a heart, teaching a quill, longer readings a scroll, and the Library an archive box. Gold is reserved for the daily quotation card; the other categories use separate green, blue, rose, teal, copper, violet and slate accents. Both card edges remain framed.

Saints of charity sharing a date appear together in one card, preserving both entries without duplicating the card color or icon. The creation quotation and daily quotation are selected from separate pools; an economic quotation cannot repeat the exact daily quotation entry.

The medieval teaching was complete in the data. Rendering split its introduction from its list of six teaching points and scheduled those pieces on separate days. Daily teaching now renders the original complete article, including lists, paragraphs and source notes. It has no inaccessible part counter.

Saved readings are grouped by author, work and URL. Numbered parts are validated, ordered and reunited. All 159 bundled parts form 23 complete saved readings, preserving all 1,706 saved paragraphs. Missing, duplicate, inconsistent or malformed groups are excluded from the daily rotation. Their source link remains available in the Library.

A longer daily reading can be expanded immediately in the same card to read its remaining saved text. No continuation is held for a later date. Library → Readings provides all complete saved passages together, including their endings. This describes the saved text, not a claim that every source's footnotes or every chapter of a book was imported.

Validation: 37 local regression tests pass. Browser checks cover unique computed colors/icons, matching borders, the full medieval article, first and last saved reading paragraphs, same-card continuation, multiple saints and the existing mobile/desktop accessibility audit. App/service-worker version: ora-v213.

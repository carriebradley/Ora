# Byzantine Saint of the Day: December 7–10, 2026 (v293)

Four original short biographies have been added to the offline Saint of the Day card. Each is at most 50 words.

Assignments in the two Catholic calendars already imported into Ora:
- December 7 — Ambrose of Milan (Edmonton and Melkite Newton).
- December 8 — Patapius / Patapios (Edmonton and Newton).
- December 9 — Conception of Mary by Saint Anna / Maternity of Ann (Edmonton and Newton). The original feast title remains intact. The short Saint Anna biography uses a narrowly bounded match, not the Marian feast title alone.
- December 10 — Martyr Menas (Mena), first listed among Menas, Hermogenes and Eugraphus; Melkite also notes beginning of the shorter Nativity Fast. The shorter-fast information is not overwritten.

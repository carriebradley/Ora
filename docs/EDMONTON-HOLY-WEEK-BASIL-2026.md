# Edmonton Ukrainian Holy Week 2026: cathedral appointments and two Paschal cycles

Reviewed November 16, 2026.

## Primary evidence

The Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Edmonton's 2026 Easter program details Holy Week services at its Alberta parishes.

Official source: https://eeparchy.com/Easter%20Program%20.pdf

St. Josaphat Cathedral in Edmonton lists **April 2 (Great Thursday)** with Vespers, the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil and washing of feet. It lists **April 4 (Great Saturday)** with Vespers followed by the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil. Its Pascha is April 5. St. Nicholas and Exaltation of the Holy Cross likewise list Vespers with St. Basil on April 4.

**Important limitation:** the same eparchial program places **St. George Parish** on Great Thursday April 9, Great Saturday April 11 and Pascha April 12. The nationwide UGCC Patriarchal Liturgical Commission 2026 calendar also places Pascha on April 12: https://ugcc.ua/data/tserkovnyy-kalendar-ugkts-na-2026-rik-8059/ . The two cycles coexisted in the eparchy in 2026.

## Ora policy

Ora's Ukrainian/Edmonton selection currently follows the April 5 Paschal cycle. Its April 2 and 4 services now identify **Vesperal Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great**, as documented by the cathedral and eparchial program. This does not imply every Edmonton parish had those dates or that Ora verifies a universal eparchial calendar.

The source and local-cycle caveat are kept under the collapsed **Readings & propers** panel, outside the spoken prayers. The private Ukrainian Basil book slot is retained. No service start time, Scripture reference or variable hymn is inferred. April 9/11 remain **calculated**, rather than being falsely verified by a different parish's April 12 Paschal calendar. Melkite remains independent.

Consider an explicit Ukrainian parish or Paschal-calendar option for a future release, but do not silently change the current April 5 mode without an explicit choice.

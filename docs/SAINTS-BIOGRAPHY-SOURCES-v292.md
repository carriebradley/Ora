# Source-backed Byzantine Saint of the Day: December 1–6, 2026 (v292)

Calendar authority is the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Edmonton (`edmonton-2026.js`), and for the Melkite mode the Diocese of Newton's 2026 typikon (`melkite-2026.js`, December p. 14).

Eight newly written biographies, each of no more than 50 words, follow the appointed Roman calendar and have separate historical source links. The named first saint receives the brief card for a shared memorial; all saints remain in the Calendar.

**Historical cautions:** The canonical Gospels do not name Joachim and Anne; those names come from early Christian tradition. Mary Magdalene should not be conflated with the unnamed sinful woman in Luke 7. The accounts make these distinctions explicit.

The saints' dates are fixed in the Roman calendar; higher Sundays can take precedence. In 2027, the Sunday of July 25 takes precedence over James's feast, so the card is not promoted. No rites, prayers, or lectionary entries are changed.

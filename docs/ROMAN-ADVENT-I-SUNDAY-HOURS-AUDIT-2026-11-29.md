# First Sunday of Advent: Roman Sunday Hours, November 29, 2026

This dated comparison advances [issue #160](https://github.com/carriebradley/Ora/issues/160). The [2027 U.S. liturgical calendar](https://www.usccb.org/resources/2027cal.pdf), printed p. 11, places the First Sunday of Advent on November 29, 2026, in Psalter Week I. Under [General Instruction of the Liturgy of the Hours §204](https://divineoffice.org/general-instructions/), Sunday opens with Evening Prayer I on Saturday.

The comparison uses the reusable Hour bodies. A heading elsewhere on the source site can reflect the current date. All three selected Ora records have distinct Gospel canticle antiphons, with each one printed before and after its canticle. They share the Sunday Collect. The renderer uses the liturgical records for these parts instead of the devotional and weekday fallbacks.

The dated tests cover the Boston Thursday Ascension setting, the three psalm sets, reading references and distinct antiphons. The browser check opens Saturday Evening Prayer, advances to Sunday with Ora’s date control, and switches to Morning Prayer. The test uses the existing text records. The hymns on this screen remain labeled historical English, and the short readings use labeled Douay–Rheims alternatives. Exact wording throughout the three Hours still needs comparison with the current approved printed edition.

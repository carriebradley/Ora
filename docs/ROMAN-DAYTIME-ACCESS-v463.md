# Roman Daytime Prayer navigation — Ora v463

Issue [#162](https://github.com/carriebradley/Ora/issues/162). **Source-linked access only, not an internal complete Office.**

Under **Today’s Prayers → Roman**, expand **Daytime Prayer · Terce / Sext / None** to open the three separate Hours for the currently selected date. These external Universalis pages require internet and may use wording or local options different from Ora's selected Roman calendar. Opening the section does not change Ora's selected prayer, preferences or date and does not affect the existing Morning/Evening/Night Prayer or Office of Readings.

**Sources and verification:** [USCCB description of the Daytime Hours](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgy-of-the-hours), [General Instruction, §§74–83](https://www.catholicculture.org/culture/library/view.cfm?recnum=5138), [Universalis explanation of current and complementary psalmody](https://universalis.com/n-liturgy-daytime.htm), and previously accessible dated [U.S. Terce page for 2026-11-24](https://universalis.com/usa/20261124/terce.htm). The pattern `/{usa|usa.boston}/YYYYMMDD/{terce|sext|none}.htm` provides a date-specific destination; the January 11, 2027 page content itself has **not** been independently verified against the approved First Edition. The Boston path is selected only when Ora's Ascension setting is Thursday. Other diocesan/custom options need review on Universalis.

**Limitations:** Ora does not supply the appointed Daytime Hour hymn, psalmody, antiphons, reading, verse or collect inside the PWA, nor offline. Do not treat the linked webpage's alternative English translations as certified U.S. First Edition wording. The General Instruction instructs a single Hour to use current psalmody and more than one Hour to use complementary psalmody for additional Hours.

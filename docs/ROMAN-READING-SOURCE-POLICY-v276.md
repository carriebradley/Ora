# Roman Office second-reading source policy — v276

Ora prioritizes the exact appointed nonbiblical reading in the current First Edition English Office, where source boundaries and reproduction permission can be verified. Next, a complete excerpt from an identifiable historical English translation may be used when the original work and numbered sections match. An approximate translation must be labelled as an alternative, never presented as the verbatim ICEL excerpt. A full historical work may be offered as a supplement, not falsely described as the exact modern Office passage.

Primary checks: the ICEL publication policy and First Edition witness; the Holy See's English documents at vatican.va for historical context and full liturgical documents when available; and public-domain patristic collections for alternative translations. Vatican speeches quoting a few lines from a Father are not themselves a complete appointed Office reading, and the existence of a Vatican webpage does not establish permission to reproduce its entire modern English translation.

The indexed author and work, section numbers, source boundaries, attribution, and (where relevant) responsory must be checked before an entry is added to oortext.json. Do not assume that a rotating divineoffice.org page's URL alone proves the correct appointed date; inspect the actual Second reading heading and first-reading context. No incomplete or unverified passage should be added just to increase the count.

Reading UI keeps the prayer uninterrupted: only the passage, optional responsory and required brief ICEL notices remain visible. Longer provenance is available through the closed "Translation and source" disclosure. Required acknowledgments remain visible at the beginning and end of ICEL reading panels.

## Additional Catholic research repositories

Search these alongside ICEL and the Holy See's [English documents](https://www.vatican.va/content/vatican/en.html). Repositories are **discovery and comparison sources**: a site's accessible text is not automatically an authorized redistribution, nor necessarily the appointed translation.

| Repository | Use in Ora research | Main caution |
|---|---|---|
| [CatholicLibrary.org](https://catholiclibrary.org/) | Broad discovery across Scripture, patristics, magisterial writings, councils, devotional and reference literature | Match each text to a named work, section and edition |
| [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/) | Patristic English translations, Catholic Encyclopedia and Aquinas | Old translations may differ from the appointed ICEL passage |
| Bibliotheca Catholica | Find primary-source bibliographies, papal texts and scans | Confirm the specific catalog/site, source edition and actual file before import |
| [EWTN Catholic Library](https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/library) | Discover papal, conciliar, hagiographical and devotional documents | Verify who owns the English translation |
| [Documenta Catholica Omnia](https://www.documentacatholicaomnia.eu/) | Locate Latin and other original-language historical witnesses and PDFs | A Latin source alone does not supply an English translation |
| Traditional Catholic Books | Locate historical prayer books, spiritual classics and scans | Confirm the actual provider URL, edition and translator |
| Saints' Books | Locate historical saint biographies, devotions and books | Confirm the actual provider URL and publication date |
| [Christian Classics Ethereal Library](https://www.ccel.org/) | Search structured Church Fathers and historical English translations | Check translation date and section numbering |
| [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) | Search downloadable older English spiritual classics | Check editorial omissions and original publication |
| [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) | Compare digitized editions of historical books and Office sources | Borrowable or viewable works may not be redistributable |

For less distinctive names (Bibliotheca Catholica, Traditional Catholic Books, Saints' Books), verify the canonical website address before using it; do not assume similarly named websites are the same project.

### Research sequence

1. Search for the exact Office date, author, work and numbered paragraphs in ICEL/First Edition witnesses. Consult Vatican English where an authentic original document or verified quotation helps corroborate the identification.
2. If the exact Office wording cannot be verified, locate the corresponding original sections through CatholicLibrary.org, New Advent, CCEL, EWTN, Documenta Catholica Omnia, or a reputable catalog.
3. Compare historical English translations from those archives, Project Gutenberg and Internet Archive against the stated sections, noting any missing sentences, modernizations or expanded context.
4. Publish only a substantiated excerpt with rights/provenance confirmed. Label a different translation as an **alternative translation**; label a whole work as a **supplement**, not the appointed Office excerpt. Keep full bibliographic notes in the collapsed source disclosure.

The Four Crowned Martyrs' precise identities remain disputed; do not merge all competing historical groups into one asserted biography. Saturninus and Sisinnius are martyrs at **Rome**, not the distinct St. Saturninus of Toulouse. St. Thomas is commemorated on the Sarum December 21 appointment; the modern Western date differs.

The Saint of the Day component already filters to the *primary observed* Western feast, so a date falling on a higher Sunday keeps the Sunday as the Liturgical Day rather than falsely promoting a displaced memorial.

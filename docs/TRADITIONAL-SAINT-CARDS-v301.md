# Traditional Saint of the Day coverage — Ora v301

Additional entries follow the **existing Sarum calendar**, and do not change its observance or ranks. All cards are 50 words or fewer and retain source links.

| Traditional date | Commemoration | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 8 | Four Crowned Martyrs | https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06163a.htm |
| Nov 9 | St. Theodore of Amasea (Tiro) | https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14573a.htm |
| Dec 21 | St. Thomas the Apostle | https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14658b.htm |

The Four Crowned Martyrs' precise identities remain disputed; do not merge all competing historical groups into one asserted biography. The November 29 Sarum kalendar commemorates Saturninus and Sisinnius, but Advent takes precedence; Ora does **not** create an unreachable primary Saint of the Day card for them. They remain in Calendar. St. Thomas is commemorated on the Sarum December 21 appointment; the modern Western date differs.

The Saint of the Day component already filters to the *primary observed* Western feast, so a date falling on a higher Sunday keeps the Sunday as the Liturgical Day rather than falsely promoting a displaced memorial.

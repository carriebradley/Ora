# Edmonton Christmas Eve 2026: source review

Reviewed: November 19, 2026.

## Evidence

- The Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Edmonton's [December 2026 calendar](https://www.eeparchy.com/calendar?month=12&year=2026) says on December 24 that the Divine Liturgy is not served according to the Typikon. It does not explain the relationship between this statement and an evening Nativity celebration.
- The [UGCC Patriarchal Liturgical Commission's 2026 calendar](https://ugcc.ua/data/tserkovnyy-kalendar-ugkts-na-2026-rik-8059/) lists Royal Hours and Vespers with the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil for December 24. This is a national source and is not by itself proof of an Edmonton-wide appointment.
- [St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Parish in Edmonton](https://stnicholasucp.ca/event/christmas-eve-divine-liturgy-of-st-basil-3/) lists Great Compline at 9 p.m. and a Christmas Divine Liturgy of St. Basil at 10 p.m. on December 24, 2026. The parish does not describe that service as Vespers with Divine Liturgy. The [eparchy's program guide](https://eeparchy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Program-Guide-Winter-Spring-2026-compressed.pdf) independently identifies St. Nicholas at the same Edmonton address.

The parish's evening schedule is evidence of one local celebration, not a general eparchial direction or evidence that its 10 p.m. Liturgy is joined to Vespers. The eparchial and parish statements may refer to different liturgical observances or periods of the civil day; the sources reviewed do not establish that explanation. Do not assume they are directly contradictory in their intended scope, or treat them as reconciled without clarification.

## Ora handling

Continue to mark the Ukrainian Edmonton December 24 service as a source conflict, without automatically selecting a service, inserting dated hymns, or applying the Melkite appointment. Keep the existing national and eparchial source links available. This review does not change the app, private books, prayer text, or service selection.

To resolve the appointment, seek an explicit eparchial explanation of what its no-Liturgy calendar note covers, and whether it authorizes or prescribes Vespers joined to St. Basil for Edmonton on this date. A parish schedule can establish that parish's stated service only. Until then, keep source-specific claims and the remaining uncertainty distinct.

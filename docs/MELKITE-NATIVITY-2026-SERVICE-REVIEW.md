# December 24, 2026 - Melkite Nativity service appointment

Reviewed: November 16, 2026.

## Official Melkite source evidence

The Diocese of Newton's **The Feast of the Nativity of Our Lord, God and Savior, Jesus Christ** (Educational Services) sets out three cases depending on Christmas weekday. Where December 25 is neither Sunday nor Monday, December 24 is the Paramony: **no morning Liturgy**; the Great Hours and Typica precede **Vespers combined with the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil**. December 25, 2026 is a Friday and falls under this first case.

- Source: https://melkite.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/The-Feast-of-the-Nativity-of-Our-Lord-God-and-Savior-Jesus-Christ.pdf
- Corroboration: https://melkite.org/faith/sunday-before-nativity

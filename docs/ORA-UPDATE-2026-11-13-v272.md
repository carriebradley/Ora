# Ora v272 — Advent Office of Readings

Added thirteen First Edition second readings and responsories from the explicit DivineOffice witnesses listed in `tools/import_oor_icel.py`: Advent I Monday and Wednesday–Saturday; Advent II Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday; Advent III Sunday, Tuesday and Friday. These follow the existing temporal index; saints and dated December propers retain their existing precedence. No calendar appointment or prayer layout was changed.

The bundle now contains 84 readings, including 20 ICEL translations used under the published free noncommercial website policy with existing first/last-frame notices. All 64 previously completed historical readings are preserved. The inventory has 562 unique references: 84 available, 478 missing. These are reference counts, not a complete annual Office.

The William of Saint Thierry witness for Advent III Monday remains deferred: its text contains an apparent transcription error (“pople”). It requires a second witness before import. Existing source punctuation is otherwise retained, including the Augustine Advent III Friday witness’s unusual sentence punctuation. No Scripture bodies, hymns or recordings were copied.

The importer now accepts bounded incremental batches and refuses to overwrite a different existing reading. The worker/app build advances together to v272 so previously installed copies receive the expanded offline bundle.

Validation: 123 unit checks pass, including original-reading fingerprints, Advent index matching, reading/responsory boundaries, installation and replacement of an older cached reading bundle. Browser cases add eight actual Advent dates, including Sunday precedence over Lucy on December 13. Local Chromium cannot start because the environment denies its process-singleton socket; the GitHub browser suite must pass before promotion to main.

# Roman Saint of the Day — January (Ora v308)

Five original, sourced saint cards of at most 50 words have been added. The app's Roman calendar controls dates and precedence; the links below supply only the biographies.

| Roman date | Primary saint card | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 2 | Basil the Great (commemorated with Gregory Nazianzen) | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-basil-the-great/ |
| Jan 4 | Elizabeth Ann Seton | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-elizabeth-ann-seton/ |
| Jan 5 | John Neumann | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-john-neumann/ |
| Jan 17 | Anthony of Egypt, Abbot | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-anthony-of-egypt/ |
| Jan 21 | Agnes of Rome | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-agnes/ |

Saint Basil is first in the combined January 2 memorial and therefore the primary short card. When a Sunday or a higher feast takes precedence, the saint biography is not promoted over it. No calendar rules, Scripture or liturgical translations were altered.

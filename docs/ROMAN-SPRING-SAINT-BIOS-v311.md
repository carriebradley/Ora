# Roman Saint of the Day — Spring (v311)

Six original, succinct Roman saint biographies are available when the selected Roman calendar actually observes the saint. Each is 50 words or fewer; a separate Catholic source supports the historical account.

- March 19: Saint Joseph, Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary — https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-joseph-husband-of-mary/
- April 25: Saint Mark, Evangelist — https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-mark/
- April 29: Saint Catherine of Siena — https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-catherine-of-siena/
- May 2: Saint Athanasius — https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-athanasius/
- May 3: Saints Philip and James (primary card for Philip) — https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saints-philip-and-james/
- May 14: Saint Matthias — https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-matthias/

The fixed-date biographies never override a higher temporal observance. The 2027 Easter Sunday on April 25 displaces Saint Mark; Sunday May 2 displaces Athanasius. Saint Joseph remains a Solemnity in Lent.

No calendar rules, readings, or prayer texts have been altered.

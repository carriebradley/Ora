**Excluded:** `Augustine|On Psalm 33, 29` is not mapped to the New Advent Psalm 33 page: the historical translation displayed there ends at section 22, so the requested section 29 cannot be checked from that witness. The app retains its original work-specific external search for this reference.

New Advent publishes a nineteenth-century translation (J. E. Tweed in the *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, 1888). Its historical numbering may differ from the Office excerpt and from modern Bible verse numeration. Ora labels all such links as historical **source editions**, not complete official readings, and the Office availability inventory stays unchanged.

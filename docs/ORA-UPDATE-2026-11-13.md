# Ora v270 — liturgical reader and office additions

## Appearance

More includes Light, Dark and System buttons. Choices persist on the device, apply before first paint, and cover the complete app, including native controls and liturgical colors. System follows device changes immediately.

## Reader

Repair the long tracked Melkite Chrysostom title across up to six physical PDF rows; render it once in normal title case. Recognize joint priest/deacon ceremonial directions as rubrics, hidden by default. Use a text-presentation cross and restrained centered serif headings. Speaker labels and responses retain distinct styling. The private book remains entirely on the device.

## Roman Office

The reproducible audit now loads the same imported saints as the app, including official optional-memorial rank corrections for Anselm, Christopher Magallanes and Ephrem. For November 12, 2026–June 30, 2027 with Sunday Epiphany, Ascension and Corpus Christi, all 462 morning/evening offices have antiphons, responsory, Gospel antiphon, intercessions, concluding prayer and an appointed short reading available. This measures section availability, not certification of every textual detail or completeness of all four offices. See the accompanying JSON report and tools/audit_roman_office_texts.cjs.

New witnesses include fixed feasts, imported-calendar memorials, Easter VI Friday before/after Ascension, and Easter VII Sunday. Easter Octave Saturday now anticipates the Second Sunday of Easter. Easter memorial psalmody retains seasonal antiphons. The Mother of the Church uses USCCB-confirmed 2022 Gospel antiphons rather than earlier common substitutions. Sources and editorial corrections are recorded per record.

Eligible First Edition ICEL prayers and approved concluding prayers are reproduced with acknowledgments. Scripture remains separately sourced from public-domain Douay–Rheims, with LXX2012 Judith 8:25–27 to match the Greek numbering; Joel's numbering/whole-verse expansion is identified. Modern Grail psalm translations, NAB Scripture and protected hymn lyrics are not incorporated by these additions.

Remaining: the complete annual Office of Readings cycle, all approved hymn texts and official modern psalm translations; the full alternate local-transfer calendar matrix is not certified. In particular, Second Sunday after Christmas with fixed January 6 Epiphany and Ordinary Sunday IX with Thursday Corpus Christi require further source checking. Default-calendar coverage must not be generalized to every setting.

## Byzantine prayer companion

Added the Small Doxology, Creed without the Filioque, and Prayer of the Hours from Shann's public-domain Euchology (1891), with explicit historical-translation attribution. These supplement ordinary Small Compline. Bright Week omits the ordinary fixed additions and points to the Paschal order.

Remaining: complete daily/seasonal canons, troparia, kontakia, remaining Compline closing prayers, and full Orthros/Vespers/Midnight Office/First–Ninth Hour books in the selected churches' current approved English editions. The app remains labelled a prayer companion.

## November Melkite typicon

User-supplied November-2026.docx, compiled by Rev. Protodeacon Saleem Naber, Christ the Savior Melkite Catholic Church, Yonkers, with outline by Rev. Peter Boutros. Imported factual St. John Chrysostom appointments on November 1, 8, 13, 14, 15, 16, 21, 22, 25, 26, 29 and 30 only, plus Orthros references where supplied. No other date or Ukrainian appointment is inferred from this source. The document also contains hymn orders and bilingual texts; those are not represented as complete daily Hours.

Conflicts are exposed in Readings: November 1 epistle differs from Newton's annual calendar; November 26 has the invalid Luke 18:34–34; November 29 extends the annual calendar's Luke 18:18–27 to verse 28. Annual-calendar references are retained pending clarification. November 22 Luke 12:16–22 is unusual but agrees with both supplied sources and is preserved as printed.

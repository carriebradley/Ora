# Roman Mass and Hours across three calendar boundaries

[Issue #164](https://github.com/carriebradley/Ora/issues/164). The [USCCB 2027 calendar](https://www.usccb.org/resources/2027cal.pdf), printed pp. 7, 11–12 and 23, supplies the dates, daytime observances and Mass references. [Universal Norms §§60–61](https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Calendar/Info/GNLY.pdf) govern transfers and the precedence of Evening Prayer.

| Civil date | Today and Roman Mass | Gospel for the selected date | Evening Prayer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 29, 2026 | First Sunday of Advent | Mark 13:33–37 | The Sunday keeps Evening Prayer II. St. Andrew's weekday feast starts the following day. [Evening Prayer witness](https://divineoffice.org/adv-w01-sun-ep2/) |
| Nov 30, 2026 | St. Andrew, Apostle, feast | Matthew 4:18–22 | St. Andrew's own Evening Prayer. [Evening Prayer witness](https://divineoffice.org/1130-ep2/) |
| Dec 7, 2026 | St. Ambrose, memorial | Luke 5:17–26 | The Immaculate Conception takes precedence at Evening Prayer I. [Evening Prayer witness](https://divineoffice.org/1208-ep1/?accessible=true&date=20251207) |
| Dec 8, 2026 | Immaculate Conception, solemnity | Luke 1:26–38 | The Immaculate Conception, Evening Prayer II. [Evening Prayer witness](https://divineoffice.org/1208-ep2/) |
| Apr 4, 2027 | Second Sunday of Easter, Divine Mercy Sunday | John 20:19–31 | The Second Sunday of Easter keeps Evening Prayer II over the following solemnity. [Evening Prayer witness](https://divineoffice.org/easter-w02-sun-ep2/) |
| Apr 5, 2027 | Annunciation of the Lord, transferred from March 25 | Luke 1:26–38 | The Annunciation, Evening Prayer II. [Evening Prayer witness](https://divineoffice.org/0325-ep2-annunciation/) |

At each boundary the browser uses Ora’s next-date control, opens the Roman Mass through More, follows its Readings button, and checks Evening and Night Prayer. It verifies each date's Today title and rank, the Mass reader's date, rank and season, the Gospel citation and dated USCCB link, and the evening Office's reading reference and First or Second Vespers context.

The readings fixture contains only published citations and calendar metadata. The comparison concerns the date-only Mass preview, the U.S. calendar defaults and the Office precedence. A particular evening Mass can follow different permitted arrangements. Live feed coverage for those dates, local propers and exact approved prayer wording require separate verification.

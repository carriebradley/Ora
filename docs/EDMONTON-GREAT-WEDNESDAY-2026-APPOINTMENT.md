# Great Wednesday 2026 - verified Edmonton Presanctified appointment

Verified: November 16, 2026.

The Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Edmonton's dated calendar page for April 1, 2026 explicitly describes Great Wednesday as an aliturgical day for the **Eucharistic Divine Liturgy**, but the same page provides the **Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts on Wednesday evening**.

Sources:
- https://eeparchy.com/calendar/2026-04-01 (dated Edmonton entry, with Wednesday evening Presanctified service and its propers)
- https://eeparchy.com/uk/calendar/2026-04-01 (accessible equivalent date entry)
- https://eeparchy.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/03/Bulletin-2019-03-10-Dormition.pdf (Edmonton bulletin explaining that the Presanctified Gifts are given from previously sanctified Eucharist, without a new consecration)

Thus the notice that the Divine Liturgy is not celebrated does **not** mean there is no Presanctified service. Ora now labels the selected Ukrainian Presanctified service on this date as **verified**, while retaining the same text and English service-book slot. No ordinary Chrysostom or Basil Eucharistic Liturgy is implied.

This is narrowly scoped to the dated **Ukrainian Edmonton** appointment. The Melkite April 1 service remains calculated until independently verified from its own church's sources. It does not override the unresolved Edmonton December 24 source conflict or validate every Lenten weekday.

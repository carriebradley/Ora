The Lawrence summary distinguishes early martyrdom evidence from later accounts of his charity. On 2027 August 29, Sunday displaces the Passion of John the Baptist.

August 6 Transfiguration and August 15 Assumption are feasts rather than occasions for an unrelated saint biography. Liturgical rules and source Scripture/prayer text remain unchanged.

# Edmonton / UGCC Christmas Eve 2026 service discrepancy

Reviewed: November 16, 2026

## Primary evidence and exact scope

The **Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Edmonton** dated calendar for Thursday, December 24, 2026 says: “According to the Typikon, the Divine Liturgy is not served today.” The same page contains a generic **Divine Liturgy** section with troparion and kontakion; that section heading does not itself establish that a service is appointed. The date has no Epistle or Gospel readings on the Edmonton page.

- Edmonton day: https://www.eeparchy.com/calendar/2026-12-24
- Edmonton December overview: https://www.eeparchy.com/calendar?month=12&year=2026

The **UGCC Patriarchal Liturgical Commission** official *Church Calendar for 2026*, in December 24 (Thursday), appoints **Vespers with the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great** (Вечірня з Літургією св. Василія Великого) with Hebrews 1:1–12 and Luke 2:1–20. This is a direct national church-level source, not a merely calculated standard Byzantine cycle.

- UGCC 2026 national calendar: https://ugcc.ua/data/tserkovnyy-kalendar-ugkts-na-2026-rik-8059/

This constitutes a real conflict between the Edmonton dated text and the national order. It may reflect a typikon wording mistake, a local pastoral exception, or the distinction between morning Eucharistic service and Vesperal Basil; none of these hypotheses has been confirmed by the eparchy.

## Safe application behavior

For the **Ukrainian/Edmonton** selection on December 24, 2026, retain `id: unknown` and `verification: source-conflict`. Do not automatically select a Basil book or insert any dated Ukrainian hymns from the Edmonton generic section. Keep the two official sources linked in the collapsed **Readings & propers** section, after the main prayer reading, and show a brief factual explanation there. The ordinary (source-verification) link remains Edmonton, not the national source.

**Do not transfer this uncertainty to Melkite.** Newton's separate Nativity service book appoints Vesperal Basil, and Ora already treats its December typicon's inconsistent Chrysostom heading as a different, independently documented source conflict.

The 2026 discrepancy must not be projected onto 2027 or later years.

## Resolution requirement

A corrected Edmonton dated page, an eparchial service instruction, or direct authoritative confirmation is needed before resolving the locally selected service. The national order alone does not justify silently overriding Edmonton-first mode. Keep both alternatives distinguishable in the source review until resolved.

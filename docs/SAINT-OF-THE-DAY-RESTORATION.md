# Saint of the Day card — November 14 regression fix

The Today page already included a Saint of the Day component beneath The Liturgical Day, but the data dictionary only contained a few dates. Missing date entries made the entire card disappear.

For November 14 in Ukrainian and Melkite Byzantine calendars, restore a concise card about the Holy Apostle Philip, with a cited description and no speculative icon or image. Both calendars retain their separate liturgical data. See the [Byzantine Catholic Metropolitan Cantor Institute](https://mci.archpitt.org/liturgy/November.html), the [Melkite Typikon](https://melkitetypikon.org/), and [Ukrainian Greek Catholic parish calendar](https://pokrova.org/calendar.html).

When another day has an explicitly named saint but no verified biography, show a short, clearly described placeholder rather than hiding the card or inventing a biography. Do not show a saint card for purely seasonal or ordinary weekdays without a named saint. Keep the same cream/burgundy design and position directly below The Liturgical Day.

# Melkite Holy Week 2026 - Vesperal Basil service verification

Reviewed: November 16, 2026

## Evidence

The Diocese of Newton's published Holy Week explanation, *Behold, the Bridegroom Is Coming*, explicitly prescribes the **Vesper-Liturgy of St Basil** for Great and Holy Thursday and describes the Vesper Liturgy of St Basil for Holy Saturday. The diocese's Holy Week liturgical-book catalog likewise identifies Vespers with St Basil for the Holy Thursday and Holy Saturday celebrations.

- Official Melkite Holy Week service explanation: https://melkite.org/faith/sunday-scriptures/behold-the-bridegroom-is-coming
- Official Melkite Holy Week traditions: https://melkite.org/faith/faith-worship/holy-week-traditions
- Official Diocese of Newton Holy Week books (Volumes I-II): https://store.melkite.org/product/great-holy-week-set/

For Gregorian 2026, Great Thursday is **April 2**, Great Saturday is **April 4**, and Pascha is **April 5**.

## Ora treatment

The Melkite selection on April 2 and April 4 now reads **Vesperal Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great**, retains the existing `melkite-basil` device-local book slot, and is marked `verified` as to the church's prescribed service type. The link to Newton's detailed order appears only in collapsed **Readings & propers**. It is **not** a verified parish start time.

No variable hymn text or readings were added or inferred, and no stored liturgical book was changed. The Ukrainian and Ruthenian appointments remain independent; the Ukrainian dates were not promoted to `verified` by a Melkite source.

The adjacent Great Wednesday, Good Friday and Pascha service rules are unchanged. This review covers only these two dates and the Vesperal nature of the service.

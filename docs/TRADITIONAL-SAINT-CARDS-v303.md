# Ora v303 — Reuse verified saints on Traditional feast octaves

Ora now shares the existing short, source-backed Roman biographies for Saint Martin of Tours and Saint Andrew the Apostle with the corresponding Sarum appointments. This does not modify any feast day, source text, or historical calendar rank.

| Traditional/Sarum date | Primary candidate | Reused source |
|---|---|---|
| November 11 | Martinmas, Saint Martin of Tours | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-martin-of-tours/ |
| November 18 | Octave Day of Saint Martin | Same verified Martin biography |
| December 7 | Octave Day of Saint Andrew | https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01471a.htm |

Each biography is displayed **only when its saint appears in the actual primary Sarum title** for the selected year. A higher-ranking Sunday, Vigil, or other observance still takes precedence, with displaced saints remaining in Calendar. Existing Roman date behavior is unchanged. The biography summaries remain below 50 words and are original paraphrases.

Regression coverage: `tests/saints-bio-source.test.cjs`.

# Roman Saint of the Day: February (Ora v310)

The Roman mode adds six original concise saint biographies (each no more than 50 words) and external source links. Feast dates and precedence remain set by `calendar.js` and the USCCB Roman calendar, not by the biographies.

| Date | Primary card | Biography source |
|---|---|---|
| Feb 5 | Agatha of Sicily | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-agatha/ |
| Feb 6 | Paul Miki, first of 26 martyrs of Japan | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-paul-miki-and-companions/ |
| Feb 10 | Scholastica | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-scholastica/ |
| Feb 14 | Cyril, first of Saints Cyril and Methodius | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saints-cyril-and-methodius/ |
| Feb 22 | Peter the Apostle, Feast of the Chair of Saint Peter | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/chair-of-saint-peter/ |
| Feb 23 | Polycarp of Smyrna | https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-polycarp/ |

**Special precedence in 2027:** USCCB's official `2027cal.pdf` states February 10 is Ash Wednesday, February 14 is the First Sunday of Lent, and February 23 is a Lenten weekday. The St. Scholastica, Cyril-and-Methodius, and Polycarp biographies do not override those primary observances. February 22 remains the Feast of the Chair of Saint Peter.

USCCB annual calendar: https://www.usccb.org/resources/2027cal.pdf

Franciscan Media's editorial Saint of the Day dates can differ from a liturgical calendar; its pages are **biographical sources only**, never authoritative date overrides. No copyrighted liturgical texts are reproduced.

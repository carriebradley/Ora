## St. Martin's Lent

The old calculation combined two different questions: whether a date falls within a historical observance, and whether someone fasts on that particular day. It omitted Sundays and Christmas Eve from its St. Martin's label and described almost every remaining day as a fast. Today also omitted the detailed calendar card entirely.

The proposed behavior keeps the historical observance visible from Martinmas, 11 November, through Christmas Eve, 24 December. It leaves the Sarum liturgical season intact: the app can show both “After Trinity” or “Advent” and “St. Martin's Lent.” The selected tradition's calendar card now appears on Today.

Ora's existing historical notes identify the Monday, Wednesday and Friday rule associated with Mâcon (581). This is the implemented historical pattern, with Martinmas displayed as the opening feast. Sundays are identified without a historical fast; other weekdays are not automatically fasting days. Feasts retain their names and can coexist with a scheduled fast. No blanket exemption for every saint's feast is invented. Ember and vigil fasts retain their own descriptions. When Christmas Eve falls on Sunday, its vigil fast is anticipated to Saturday, including when that Saturday is also an Ember Day.

This is a historical overlay, not a claim that every medieval Sarum parish or Irish monastery followed the same winter fast. A particular Irish monastic rule would need its own evidence and selection.

The existing notes distinguish earlier Gaelic customs from Sarum practice and remain visible throughout the observance.

## Byzantine review

The original app combined a Ukrainian supplemental saints list, user-imported UGCC events for 2026–27, a small shared feast calendar, and an unofficial Slavic lectionary. That combination is useful but cannot establish complete agreement with both U.S. Ruthenian and Ukrainian practice.

The branch adds a Settings choice between U.S. Ukrainian Greek Catholic and Ruthenian practice. Ukrainian remains the default to match the original data. Gregorian Pascha and fixed feasts remain the app's current UI configuration. The Ukrainian supplemental saints and imported events are shown only for the Ukrainian selection, rather than being presented as a Ruthenian calendar.

Changes backed by the reviewed sources:

| Finding | Prepared change |
| --- | --- |
| Every Great Fast day was described as a strict fast, including Sundays | Separate the fasting season from the general U.S. daily requirement; distinguish the two church profiles |
| Preparatory Triodion weeks were labeled Great Fast | Give preparation its own description |
| Sunday tones were absent during the Triodion | Continue the tone cycle; check 50 numeric Sunday tones against MCI's 2026 calendar |
| St. Anna's conception was always dated 9 December | Keep 9 December for the Ukrainian profile; use 8 December for U.S. Ruthenian practice |
| Royal Hours were always attached to the eve itself | Move them to Friday when the eve falls on Saturday or Sunday; keep the vigil date separate |
| Sunday after Nativity could not be identified on 1 January | Include that date in the window |
| July and October Sundays of the Fathers were absent | Add the First Six and Seventh Councils commemorations in the documented Sunday windows |
| Several Ukrainian single-day fasts were absent | Identify the Nativity and Theophany eves, Exaltation, and Beheading in the Ukrainian profile |

Individual exemptions and annual dispensations are not computed.

## Church Fathers and Polycarp

The original `oortext.json` has exactly 63 completed texts. All 63 entries, including their paragraph wording and attribution, remain unchanged. A fingerprint fixture makes this check repeatable.

The missing entry was already indexed for 23 February as “Church of Smyrna — Letter on the Martyrdom of Saint Polycarp 13, 2–15, 3.” Its text was absent and its index entry did not enable “Read it here.” The branch adds the matching public-domain passage from Kirsopp Lake, beginning with preparation of the pyre and ending with the fragrance of incense. The total is now 64 texts. Optional commemorations can also open their bundled text, which matters when Polycarp falls during Lent.

Lake numbers the closing material as 15.2; the Office index uses 15.3. The source credit explains that difference. The added text is Lake's translation, not the copyrighted modern liturgical translation. Quotation marks have been normalized typographically; the original 63 texts were not edited.

Sources: [Felix Just's index of non-biblical readings](https://catholic-resources.org/LoH/OfficeOfReadings-NonBiblical-Readings.html); [Lake's Martyrdom of Polycarp](https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/martyrdompolycarp-lake.html).

## Other bugs fixed on the branch

## Validation and its limits

All 14 repeatable Node tests passed. They cover the three calendar engines on every date in 2026–2027, St. Martin's boundary dates across three years, the documented Byzantine Sunday tones, selected movable feasts and fasting boundaries, reading preservation and connection, and service-worker installation, upgrades, invalid responses and storage failures. See [test instructions](../tests/README.md).

Additional exploratory checks exercised 140 prayer-hour renderings across 14 representative dates, with no thrown errors or empty results in that sample. A six-year Byzantine citation scan identified the two empty dates listed above. These checks establish that those paths run; they do not authenticate every prayer or lectionary citation.

Browser checks use Chromium at a 390 × 844 phone viewport for all six tabs in all three traditions, exercise settings and keyboard focus, open the Polycarp commemoration, and load saved prayer texts with the network disabled. The 18 phone page checks and two desktop theme checks passed with no JavaScript errors or axe WCAG A/AA violations in the tested views. A desktop calendar check also covers light and dark themes. Screenshots and machine-readable results are saved outside the app by the browser test.

External services are blocked during the repeatable browser test to make it deterministic. Live Scripture, artwork and streamed audio, Safari/iOS installation, and an update on an already installed phone have not been certified. No deployment has been performed.
